Summary

Tiles – Merge & Purge does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The game runs entirely on your device. It has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking of any kind. It does not send anything to us or to anyone else, and it works with no network connection.

Data we collect

None. We operate no servers, and the app contains no code that sends data anywhere. We never receive your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, device identifiers, photos, or any other personal information, because none of it is ever requested or transmitted.

These values never leave your device. They are not personal data — they identify nothing about you and cannot be used to recognize you. They are readable only by this app, and they are deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

If you have enabled Android's own backup feature for your device, Android may include this app's local data in the backup that Google stores in your personal Google account. That backup is created and controlled by Android and Google, not by us, and we have no access to it. You can turn it off in your device settings under Backup.

Permissions

The app declares a single Android permission, INTERNET. It is a standard requirement of the app framework the game is built with (Capacitor) and is not used to transfer any data: the game makes no network requests of its own. Two things in the settings window do lead outward, and neither sends us anything. Rate this game opens the app's Play Store listing — your device opens it in the Play Store app or your browser. Share this game hands your device's own share sheet the app's name, its one-line description and the address of that same listing; you choose which app receives them, and we never learn which one it was or whether you shared at all. The app requests no other permission — no camera, no microphone, no location, no contacts, no storage access.

Third parties

The app integrates no advertising networks, no analytics or crash-reporting services, and no social-media components.

Distribution through Google Play is the one exception outside our control: when you download, install, or update the app, Google processes that interaction and may collect data such as installation and crash statistics on its own behalf. That processing is governed by Google's privacy policy, not by this one. See policies.google.com/privacy.

The same applies to rating the app. The game can take you to its Play Store listing, and after a particularly good round it may once ask the Play Store to show its own review dialog. Both are shown and handled by the Play Store app, not by us: we never see whether you rated the app or what you wrote, and a review you submit is processed by Google under the policy linked above. The game sends nothing of its own either way.

Sharing the app works the same way. The share sheet belongs to your device, and the app you pick from it — a messenger, your mail program, anything else — receives the text we handed over and is then governed by its own privacy policy, not by this one. We are not involved in that step and learn nothing about it.

Children

The game contains no data collection whatsoever and is therefore suitable for users of any age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and we could not do so: no information is collected from anyone.

Your rights

Because we do not process any personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export, or erase on your behalf — and no request under the GDPR, the CCPA, or comparable law can apply to data we do not hold. The values described above are under your sole control on your own device, and clearing the app's data removes them.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app introduces a feature that does process data — for example, advertising or online leaderboards — we will update this policy before that version is released and change the effective date above. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes on Google Play. The current version of this policy always applies to the version of the app you have installed.
